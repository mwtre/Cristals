Project Pitch

Crystal Remote Factory

Automated & AI-Enabled Custom Crystal Awards Production Line

Project Lead: Enzo C. · February 24, 2026

1 Project Vision – One-Liner

Transform our 100% manual crystal awards production into a smart, remote-controllable factory:

  • Customers design/upload remotely
  • AI assists with crystal pattern editing
  • Proprietary software controls robotic arms for precise etching/hatching
  • Automated gentle packing into boxes with protective foam

→ Higher throughput · Lower labor & breakage costs · New premium remote/custom revenue stream

2 Why Now?

  • Growing demand for personalized crystal awards
  • Current manual line limits capacity, quality consistency and speed
  • High labor dependency → expensive & error-prone (especially fragile handling)
  • Competitors are automating → risk of losing market share
  • Dutch government offers very strong tax incentives for exactly this kind of software + automation project

3 Phased Rollout – Low Risk, High Subsidy

Phase 1 · 3–6 months · Digital Factory Twin (our starting point)
  • Lightweight digital model / twin of the entire production line
  • Real-time tracking of every award (QR/barcode from raw blank to shipped box)
  • Live dashboard: see location, process status, bottlenecks, alerts
  • Basic remote control: pause/resume jobs, prioritize urgent customs
  • Data logging for quality & future AI decisions

→ Low cost (€5k–€15k sensors/scanners + dev hours) · Runs parallel to current manual line – no production stop · Immediate benefits: better visibility, fewer errors, data foundation · Perfect proof-of-concept before buying robots.

Phase 2 · 6–12+ months · Physical Automation
  • Add collaborative robotic arm for handling, laser etching/hatching & auto-packing
  • Gentle pick-and-place of fragile crystal awards into boxes with protective foam
  • Full integration with digital twin for seamless tracking & control
  • Later: remote edit-control app + AI design assistance

4 Recommended Automation Hardware (Phase 2 – Auto-Packing Focus)

Best fit: Collaborative robot (cobot) – safe near people, flexible for custom shapes/sizes, easy to program. Top options: Universal Robots UR10e (12.5 kg, ~1300 mm, €35k–€55k NL); Fanuc CRX-30iA (30 kg, ±0.05 mm, €50k–€80k); OMRON TM12 (12 kg, 1300 mm, built-in vision, TMFlow); local integrator Robertpack (Zwolle, NL) for custom cells with UR/Fanuc/OMRON + foam insertion.

All qualify for KIA tax deduction.

5 Tax Incentives & Financial Leverage

Why this project is financially strategic. Our Crystal Remote Factory is not just an automation upgrade — it is the development of proprietary software, digital manufacturing control systems, and AI-assisted design tools. Because of this, the project qualifies for powerful Dutch innovation tax incentives.

1. KIA – Investment deduction (hardware)

One-time extra deduction on qualifying equipment (robots, sensors, packaging machines). Reduces taxable profit in the year of investment. Helpful for small-to-medium investments; not the main driver — a bonus.

Estimated benefit
€2,000 – €4,000
on mid-sized automation (one-time)

2. WBSO – R&D payroll tax reduction

Because we develop proprietary software, WBSO applies. Qualifying: design app, AI pattern editing, Digital Twin, robot control, remote dashboard. Up to 36% reduction on R&D wage costs — significantly lowers development cost.

Estimated payroll tax relief
€30,000 – €60,000
for ~1,200 R&D hours (cash back)

3. Innovation Box – major advantage

Profits from self-developed IP taxed at 9% instead of 19–25.8%. Applies when we develop proprietary software, obtain WBSO approval, and document the IP. The long-term profit multiplier.

Tax on IP profit
9% instead of 25.8%
e.g. €168,000/year saved on €1M profit

How the 9% applies: The 9% only hits that innovation profit slice (e.g. €100,000 or whatever % your allocation justifies) — not your full revenue (e.g. €2M) or full profit (e.g. €700k). It’s the "innovation premium" part.

1. Marketing – flyers at expat companies & housing

Top-of-funnel: make sure every expat in our area knows CrystalWork exists.

  • Print and distribute 10,000 flyers at expat-heavy companies, housing complexes and relocation offices.
  • Clear value proposition: stable hours, transparent pay, clean work, growth path to machine operator / team lead.
  • QR code to a very simple application page (name, phone, WhatsApp, language, availability).
  • Option for referral: reward existing workers for bringing in reliable friends.

Goal: build a local talent pool > 100 interested candidates within 2–3 months.

2. Online – only ads with Indeed & similar

Always-on digital funnel that keeps bringing candidates without manual outreach.

  • Create 1–2 standard job profiles (Production Worker, Line Operator) and keep them live on Indeed and local boards.
  • Use clear shift windows and real pictures from the factory so expectations match reality.
  • Screen quickly: short phone or WhatsApp call, then invite for a paid trial shift.
  • Feed accepted workers directly into our production stats system (Enzo / Andre / Enrique / Scoth cards).

Goal: 3–5 hires / month purely from online channels once tuned.

3. Import workers – Spain, Italy, Poland

Structured partnership with agencies to bring stable teams, with housing & transport provided.

  • Work with trusted partners in Spain, Italy and Poland to pre-screen candidates for language, reliability and willingness to relocate.
  • CrystalWork (or partner) organises housing and daily transport to the factory.
  • All housing & transport costs are charged directly on the salary, up to 25% of net pay, so the worker has no surprises and we stay compliant.
  • Standard contract structure: minimum guaranteed hours, clear overtime rules, onboarding pack in native language.

Goal: build a stable international core team (e.g. 10–20 workers) that can cover peaks and night shifts.

How this fits the factory. Local expats and online applicants cover flexible day shifts, while imported workers provide a stable backbone for evening / night and repetitive tasks. All three channels feed into the same production stats dashboard, so we can see hours, cost per hour and performance per worker, task and work order.
